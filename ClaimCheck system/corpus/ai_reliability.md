section: Hallucination in natural language generation
updated: 2023-02-01
url: https://doi.org/10.1145/3571730
---
Large language models can generate fluent statements that are factually inconsistent with their source or the real world, commonly described as hallucinations.

Reliable evaluation should consider the task, source availability, factuality criteria, and the consequences of an incorrect generated statement.
